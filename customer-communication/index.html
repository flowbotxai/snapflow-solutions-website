COMMON QUESTIONS

Make it work for your business.

Choose the coverage and level of support that fit your day-to-day needs.

Do I need voice, text, and website chat together?+

No. Each can be set up separately. If you prefer to start with missed-call text back or website chat, we can do that without adding a voice assistant or marketing services.

Can this support my receptionist?+

Yes. You can use assistants for overflow calls, after-hours coverage, and repeated customer conversations, giving your receptionist more time for work that needs personal attention.

How does the assistant know about my business?+

We configure it with information you approve, including services, policies, service areas, and answers to common and more detailed questions. If information is missing or a request needs personal attention, we define an appropriate handoff instead of relying on a guessed answer.

Can a caller speak with a person?+

Yes. We can configure live call transfers to you or your team based on the caller’s needs and your routing preferences. If nobody is available, the assistant can collect a message and callback details.

Can it schedule appointments?+

Appointment booking can be included when your calendar and booking rules can be connected. Otherwise, the assistant can collect availability or a callback request for your team.

Can I review the conversations?+

Voice setups can include call recordings and transcripts. Text and web conversations can also be saved in connected software. We establish access, retention, and any required notices during setup.

Can I keep my existing phone number and software?+

Often, yes. We review your phone provider, messaging setup, and software before confirming the approach. Routing, texting registration, permissions, and integrations depend on your current setup.
