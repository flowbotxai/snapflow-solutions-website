COMMON QUESTIONS

Useful answers.
Clear expectations.

You don’t need to become a data analyst to understand your results.

Can I use reporting without your other services?+

Yes. Reporting can be a standalone service or part of your marketing and automation work. We review your existing tools, goals, and data before confirming the scope.

What is return on ad spend?+

Return on ad spend, or ROAS, compares revenue attributed to advertising with the amount spent on those ads. It measures revenue return, not profit; it does not subtract service delivery costs, management fees, or other business expenses.

Can you track every lead through to a sale?+

We connect the available sources and sales records where supported. Missing records, offline activity, privacy choices, and disconnected systems can leave gaps. We make those limits clear instead of presenting incomplete data as the full picture.

Do I need different software?+

Not necessarily. We first look at your current advertising, analytics, and customer management tools. We can use existing reporting or configure a dashboard around the supported connections.

Will you make improvements or just send reports?+

For services we manage, we use the findings to make and measure improvements within the agreed scope. For reporting-only work, we identify the priorities and discuss any implementation separately.
