A FEW COMMON QUESTIONS

Before we build.

Simple answers to help you decide what your website needs.

Can you improve my existing website?+

Yes. We can review your current site and identify what is worth keeping, what needs updating, and whether targeted improvements or a rebuild make the most sense.

What is the difference between a website and a landing page?+

A website explains your business and its services across several pages. A landing page focuses on one offer or purpose, such as an ad campaign, with a clear next step for the visitor.

Are your websites SEO and AI search ready?+

Yes. Our websites are built with SEO and AI search-ready foundations: useful service content, clear headings, internal links, relevant structured data, and appropriate technical structure. AI search visibility depends on many factors; no website can guarantee rankings or inclusion in AI answers.

Can you include service pages and location pages?+

Yes. Dedicated pages can explain the services you offer and the areas you genuinely serve. Each page should provide useful, specific information rather than repeat the same content with a different city name.

Can the website connect to my existing software?+

We review the tools you use and connect supported forms, scheduling, customer records, and tracking where practical. A website assistant can also be added to answer questions using your business information and collect inquiry details.

Do I have to purchase marketing or automation too?+

No. Website design, landing pages, marketing, and automation are available individually. We can connect them when they fit your goals and budget.
