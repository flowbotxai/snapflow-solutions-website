Step 3

Install into the correct location.

  1. Before purchasing, confirm that an agency administrator can install the snapshot into the intended location. Sub-account access alone does not provide installation permission.
  2. After purchase, retain your receipt and plan name. Sign in to the purchasing agency account and use the purchased snapshot in your HighLevel dashboard to push it to the agreed location.
  3. Choose a clean location for the business. Reuse an appropriate existing empty location, or create a new one only if needed. Review overlaps before importing into an active location.
  4. Confirm the delivery release with SnapFlow support before importing. The current internal QA candidate is PDR Lead-to-Job - RELEASE CANDIDATE v4, refreshed October 27, 2026, with 11 workflows, 21 tags and 113 total assets. It remains subject to release acceptance; do not activate it based on the asset count alone.
  5. Install the purchased snapshot into the agreed location and record the release name and installation date.
  6. If installing into an active location, first document existing pipelines, fields, forms, calendars and workflows. Imported assets may create duplicates.
  7. After import, confirm that the pipeline, calendar, form, custom fields, tags, custom values, workflows and AI templates are present.
  8. Do not delete similarly named assets until you know which version is connected to existing forms, websites or automations.
Step 4

Replace every placeholder and connect your services.

  • Replace the business name, main phone, website, service area and shop address.
  • Replace the photo-upload link, booking link, Google review link, privacy-policy URL and terms URL.
  • Add the correct users and assign an owner for leads, alerts and escalations.
  • Set the location time zone, business hours and calendar availability.
  • Configure appointment duration, buffers, notice rules and assigned calendars.
  • Connect the buyer-owned phone number and confirm calling and SMS routing.
  • Connect the buyer-owned email service and authenticated sending domain.
  • Review A2P registration, consent language, quiet hours, DND and STOP handling.
  • Customize the PDR Damage & Inspection Request form and embed or link it from the buyer's website. Replace the placeholder business name and the form's privacy-policy and terms links before use; do not leave REPLACE_WITH links in the public form.
  • Review every email and SMS message for accuracy, tone and approved business information.

Recommended photo instructions

Ask for a few clear photos of what the customer would like repaired. A wider view, close-up and different angles are useful suggestions. If intake was already captured by voice, request photos by reply after the call, with permission; do not send the full form again. Photos help prepare for an inspection and do not guarantee repairability or a final price.

Your shop, your process

Choose the features your business wants.

You do not need to use every workflow or offer online scheduling. Before activation, record your choices with the person who handles your leads. Leave unwanted workflows in Draft and unwanted AI features Off. Keep a copy of the original configuration before editing.

Feature choiceWhat to review before enabling or disabling it

For staff-arranged inspections, remove public booking offers and links, remove or disable the AI booking action, and change the AI instructions to collect a request for staff follow-up. Keep the handoff and owner notification configured. Pausing a confirmation workflow alone does not disable booking. Test both the public link and the voice agent.
Damage photo textsReview the Voice AI actions and PDR - Voice Intake & Photo Follow-Up, New Lead & Damage Intake, Missed Call & Intake Recovery, Incomplete Damage Intake Recovery, and form instructions. If you do not want photo requests, remove those requests and their reminders wherever used, update the AI instructions, and stop existing queued requests. Preserve contact capture and staff alerts. Test that no photo request is sent.

One intake, then only missing information.

A missed call with no captured intake may receive the full intake form. When the voice assistant has already collected the details, it must save those details and ask only for anything missing or changed. If photo texts are enabled and the caller agrees, send a photo-only request after the call ends so the customer can reply with pictures. Do not send the same customer back through the full form.

Changing a feature that is already live

  1. Record the current configuration and return the affected workflows to Draft before changing them.
  2. Review Enrollment History and pending actions for those workflows. Remove affected contacts from obsolete sequences using the supported workflow controls; do not assume a Draft switch alone has cancelled every queued message.
  3. Update the related AI actions and instructions, public links, calendar availability, message copy and internal assignments.
  4. Run a controlled test for the enabled path and the disabled path. Check messages, bookings, staff alerts and pending actions before resuming live traffic.
Need help choosing or changing a feature? Contact SnapFlow support with your feature choice and destination location. Do not share passwords or authentication codes. Ask for help if a dependency or queued action is unclear.

Step 7

Complete the launch gate.

  • No placeholder business information remains.
  • No prior-client or test customer data is present.
  • Every automation has an owner, trigger, stop condition and opt-out path.
  • Calendar assignments and internal alerts reach the correct person.
  • The full synthetic lead-to-job journey passed.
  • The buyer approved the message copy and go-live timing.
  • A rollback plan exists: return workflows to Draft and disconnect live traffic.
Publish gradually. Activate workflows individually and monitor the first tests before enabling the next stage. Do not publish every automation simultaneously.
