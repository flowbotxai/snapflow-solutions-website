COMMON QUESTIONS

Before we get started.

Can you improve an existing Google Ads account?+

Yes. We review your campaigns, search terms, landing pages, tracking, and available results before deciding what to change.

Do I need a new website?+

Not necessarily. We can use a suitable existing page or build a focused landing page. Website & Landing Page Design →

Do you manage advertising on AI platforms?+

Google Ads is the platform currently included in this offering. Other advertising platforms are assessed separately before we confirm availability, scope, and support.

How is this different from SEO and AI search visibility?+

Paid advertising buys placement. SEO and AI search work on how your business is found and understood in unpaid search experiences. SEO & AI Search Visibility →

Will you guarantee a number of leads?+

No. Demand, competition, budget, your offer, and the customer experience all affect results. We use the available evidence to guide ongoing improvements.
