COMMON QUESTIONS

Before we get started.

Clear expectations about your options, budget, and measurement.

Do I need both Google Ads and local SEO?+

No. You can choose either service on its own. Google Ads provides paid visibility; local SEO works on your presence in organic search and Google Maps. We discuss which approach fits your goals, market, and budget.

Is the advertising budget included in management fees?+

Advertising spend and management fees are separate. We discuss both before work begins so you understand what goes toward Google and what covers our services.

Can you work with my existing website or Google Ads account?+

Yes. We review what you already have, retain useful work, and identify improvements. If a campaign needs a more focused destination, we can create a landing page without rebuilding your entire website.

Can you track leads through to sales?+

Where your tools and data allow, we connect campaign sources, inquiries, and customer records to sales outcomes. Call clicks and form submissions are starting signals; qualified leads and recorded sales tell us more about business value.

Can you guarantee the top Google Maps position?+

No. Rankings vary by search, location, competition, and other factors. We work to improve your visibility and measure progress across the areas you serve, without promising a specific ranking.

How often will I receive reporting?+

We agree on a reporting schedule that fits the service and activity level. Weekly updates can highlight active changes, while monthly reviews show broader trends, results, and priorities.
