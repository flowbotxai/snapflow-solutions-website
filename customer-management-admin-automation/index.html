COMMON QUESTIONS

Make the system work for you.

Start with the tasks that take up your time—not a long list of software features.

What is customer management software?+

It’s a place to keep customer details, notes, conversations, and follow-up organized. You may hear it called a CRM. The goal is simple: know who you’re helping, what they need, and what happens next.

Do I have to switch software?+

No. We can review your current system first. If it supports the improvements you need, we can work within it. If a connection or feature is unavailable, we explain the options before making changes.

Can I start with one automation?+

Yes. You can start with intake, appointment reminders, follow-up, or another specific task. Marketing and AI voice services are separate options—not requirements.

Can you follow up with past customers?+

Yes. We can help organize customer groups and set up relevant offers, service announcements, and business updates. Messages are configured around appropriate contact permissions and unsubscribe preferences.

Will this work with my forms and communication tools?+

We review the available integrations and permissions before confirming what can connect. Some systems support direct connections; others may need an additional integration or a manual step.

How do we know the automation works?+

We test triggers, customer records, messages, timing, and handoffs before launch. We also show you how the setup works and discuss any ongoing monitoring or support as part of the scope.
