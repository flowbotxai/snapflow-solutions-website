What we usually find

Most of what’s costing you isn’t a marketing problem

When we look at an established service business, the money is rarely leaking where the owner expects. It’s almost never that nobody knows the company exists. Here’s what actually turns up, over and over:

Leads with nowhere to land
A contact form that emails an address nobody checks. Calls that ring out after five. Inquiries living in one person’s phone.
Quotes nobody chases
Estimates go out, the customer goes quiet, and the job is written off — after all the work of measuring, pricing and driving out there.
Advertising nobody can measure
Years of ad spend with no tracking behind it, so nobody can say which dollar produced which job — or which half to cut.
The owner as the system
The answer to every question lives in one head, so nothing moves at full speed unless that person is available.

Fixing these in the right order matters more than fixing them fast. We’ll show you the order.

Common questions

Straight answers

Can you help with just one project?

Yes, and plenty of owners start that way. One project is a perfectly good place to start.

Do I have to replace my software?

No. If you’re on Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan or similar and your crew knows it, we work with what you’ve got.

Will you recommend services I don’t need?

No. Sometimes the right answer is a hire, or fixing something you already have, or leaving it alone for now. We’d rather keep the relationship than sell you a package.

Do I have to change my phone number?

No. You keep your number. We set up tracking behind it so you can tell which advertising produced which call.

Am I locked into a contract?

No long-term contract. On ongoing work we ask for an initial term long enough for results to show, then it’s month to month.

Do you only work with local businesses?

Most of our clients are here in Metro Detroit, and that’s where we’re most useful. We work remotely too.
