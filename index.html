For nearly 30 years, I owned and operated my own business in the automotive reconditioning industry. SnapFlow began with me practicing what I preach—putting better marketing, customer communication, and automation systems to work in my own business.

Customers noticed. They asked who had set everything up and told me how easy the process was. They could find the information they needed, schedule appointments, and know what to expect. The systems weren’t just making my work easier—they were making the experience better for my customers.

That feedback convinced me I was onto something. I fell in love with the work and started SnapFlow Solutions to help other business owners achieve their goals through the same practical approach that improved my own business.
