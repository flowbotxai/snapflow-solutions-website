When consulting is useful

Solve the right problem before paying to solve it.

Growth problems are often connected. Weak lead volume may actually be poor positioning. Low close rates may come from response time or estimating. Capacity problems may be scheduling, documentation or pricing. We examine the system before prescribing a service.

Growth

Acquisition and sales constraints

Evaluate visibility, offer clarity, lead quality, response, qualification, follow-up and conversion to determine where revenue is being lost.

Operations

Workflow and owner dependence

Identify bottlenecks, unclear responsibilities, missing procedures and decisions that cannot move without the owner.

Economics

Priorities and capital allocation

Compare potential improvements by expected value, effort, risk, capacity and the evidence needed to judge whether they worked.

Business architecture assessment

Turn scattered symptoms into an ordered improvement plan.

The assessment follows how demand, people, process, technology and money move through the business. Findings are written in plain language, prioritized and tied to a practical next step. You can implement with Snap Flow, with another provider or internally.

01

Understand the objective

Clarify what the owner wants to change, current capacity, constraints and the cost of leaving the problem alone.

02

Map the current state

Review workflows, systems, roles, handoffs, customer experience and available operating and financial data.

03

Find root causes

Separate symptoms from constraints and test assumptions against evidence from the business.

04

Sequence the work

Define the first improvement, ownership, cost boundary, success measure and what should wait.

Decision reporting

Use numbers to guide action—not decorate a dashboard.

Revenue and acquisition

Qualified leads, booking rate, close rate, customer acquisition cost, revenue by source and pipeline health.

Operational performance

Cycle time, backlog, response time, rework, utilization, missed handoffs and the work still dependent on one person.

Review cadence

Translate trends into decisions: continue, correct, stop, document, automate, hire or leave a working process alone.
