COMMON QUESTIONS

Before we get started.

How does local SEO help contractors and home-service businesses?+

We review your service and location pages, Google Business Profile, service-area information, and the questions customers ask before hiring. Then we improve the information that helps people understand what you do and where you work. We measure search visibility and inquiries where tracking is available; rankings and lead totals are not guaranteed.

Can I choose local SEO or AI search work separately?+

Yes. We discuss your priorities and agree on a scope. Some work supports both, while monitoring and content needs differ.

Can you guarantee a Maps ranking or an AI recommendation?+

No. Search results vary by location, competition, platform, and question. We improve the underlying information and measure progress without promising placement.

Does this include paid advertising?+

No. Paid advertising is a separate service with its own management scope and advertising budget. Explore Paid Advertising →

Do I need special AI files or special schema?+

There is no universal file or schema that guarantees AI inclusion. We focus on accessible content, accurate structured data, and information that answers customer questions.

How do review requests fit in?+

We can set up requests for honest customer feedback. Automated reminders can connect with your customer management system. Customer Management & Admin Automation →
