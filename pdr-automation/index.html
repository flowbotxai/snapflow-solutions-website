What has been demonstrated

One intake. Then a simple photo reply.

In controlled testing, native Voice AI saved vehicle and damage details, sent a photo-only request after the call and received a photo reply on the same contact.

The intended customer experience

  1. Speak with the assistant and provide the details once.
  2. With permission, receive a photo request after hangup.
  3. Reply with a few clear photos.
  4. The shop reviews the information and handles the next step.

Photos help prepare for review. The system does not price repairs, guarantee repairability or replace the technician's judgment.

Yes. Setup, troubleshooting and customization services are available for separately scoped work. They are not automatically included in the standard snapshot installation price.
