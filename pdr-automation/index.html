Straight answers

Before you choose your setup.

How is access delivered?

After a successful Self Install payment, an automatic delivery email includes the protected SnapFlow PDR Automation v4 import link, Getting Started Guide and order reference. Allow a few minutes for processing. Installed for You buyers receive intake instructions; SnapFlow handles the agreed installation, so do not import it again.

What happens after I purchase Installed for You?

Your onboarding email includes the guide and asks you to reply with your PDR business details, destination HighLevel account/location, responsible staff and calendar preferences, feature choices and existing phone/email/optional AI services. We arrange authorized access securely; never email passwords or authentication codes. We review the information and access, confirm that prerequisites are complete, then begin the agreed configuration and testing. The five-business-day installation target begins after those prerequisites are complete and excludes provider approval delays. You receive a handoff and completed-work walkthrough; do not import the snapshot a second time.

Is SnapFlow PDR Automation a separate company?

No. SnapFlow PDR Automation is a product from Snap Flow Solutions. Snap Flow Solutions is responsible for the offer, communication and any separately scoped services.

Is this a monthly subscription?

The SnapFlow purchase is one time: $197 Self Install or $699 Installed for You. Your HighLevel subscription, phone, messaging, email, domain, AI and other provider charges are separate.

Does the snapshot include a working AI phone receptionist?

Native HighLevel Voice AI is optional. The snapshot includes a disabled PDR Voice AI framework and setup guidance. It requires personalization, compatible account features, service connections and testing before activation. The snapshot license does not include an AI subscription, phone number, usage billing or ongoing management. Installed for You covers agreed setup of the supplied framework when selected; provider charges remain separate.

Will it work in my existing Go High Level account?

Use an eligible HighLevel account with agency-admin installation access. A clean subaccount is recommended. Existing accounts may contain overlapping fields, workflows, calendars or pipelines; review those overlaps before import and configure the system before activation.

What support is included?

Self Install includes ongoing guide access and AI setup guidance. Human technical support, account configuration and training calls are not included. Installed for You includes the agreed configuration and testing, a completed-work walkthrough up to 45 minutes and 30 days of scoped human email help from handoff. Migration, A2P registration/remediation, custom development and ongoing management are separately quoted.

Can I use it for more than one business?

One purchase licenses one named PDR business in one HighLevel subaccount. An agency can install for that business; unrelated businesses require separate purchases or written licenses. Resale, sublicensing and redistribution are not included. Assets Protection is not a technical one-company lock. Keep the import link private.

What are the refund terms?

Self Install: no change-of-mind refund after snapshot access is delivered. Missing access, duplicate charges or defects are reviewed for correction or refund. Installed for You: full refund if cancelled before work starts; after work starts, any refund covers unused work.

Does it automatically provide estimates or quotes?

No. The system organizes damage details and photos for review and can support inspection scheduling. Repairability, estimates, prices and final quotes are created by the repair business after review or an in-person inspection.
