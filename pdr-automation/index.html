The problem it solves

The work is not only fixing dents. It is everything that happens before and after.

A lead calls, damage details and photos arrive in different places, an inspection needs scheduling, follow-up gets delayed and the completed repair never turns into a review. The snapshot is designed to give that customer journey one defined place to go.

01

Capture the inquiry

Collect contact, vehicle and damage information through a PDR-specific intake process instead of starting from an empty contact record.

02

Move the opportunity

Track each lead from new inquiry and damage review through inspection, customer decision, booking, completed repair and review follow-up in a purpose-built pipeline.

03

Follow through consistently

Use draft workflows and templates for intake, appointment reminders, post-inspection follow-up and review requests—then configure and test them for your business before publishing.

Straight answers

Before you join the waitlist.

Can I buy or install it today?

No. The snapshot is in final testing. Joining the waitlist requests launch updates and possible early-access opportunities; it does not reserve an installation, guarantee access or commit you to a purchase. We have not announced a release date.

Is SnapFlow PDR Automation a separate company?

No. SnapFlow PDR Automation is a product from Snap Flow Solutions. Snap Flow Solutions is responsible for the offer, communication and any separately scoped services.

Is this a monthly subscription?

The planned model is a one-time snapshot purchase: $297 self-install or $699 standard installation. Sales are not open yet. You remain responsible for your own HighLevel plan and phone, messaging, email, domain, AI and other provider charges.

Does the snapshot include a working AI phone receptionist?

Native HighLevel Voice AI is optional. The planned release includes a disabled PDR Voice AI template and setup/testing guidance; full release acceptance is still underway. It does not include an AI subscription, phone number, usage billing, business configuration, activation or ongoing management.

Will it work in my existing GoHighLevel account?

An eligible account and agency-admin installation access will be required. A clean sub-account is recommended; current-release import and buyer-installation acceptance are still being completed. Existing accounts may contain overlapping fields, workflows, calendars or pipelines that require a careful compatibility review before anything is published.

Is installation or customization included?

The planned $699 option covers standard installation and is limited to the included snapshot assets and standard configuration. Migration, cleanup, new custom workflows and ongoing management are scoped separately.

Can I use it for more than one business?

The planned standard package is for one named PDR company. Redistribution, resale and use for unrelated companies are outside that scope. Final licensing and eligibility terms will be provided before purchase.

Does it automatically provide estimates or quotes?

No. The system organizes damage details and photos for review and can support inspection scheduling. Repairability, estimates, prices and final quotes are created by the repair business after review or an in-person inspection.
