The problem it solves

The work is not only fixing dents. It is everything that happens before and after.

A lead calls, damage details and photos arrive in different places, an inspection needs scheduling, follow-up gets delayed and the completed repair never turns into a review. The snapshot gives that customer journey one defined place to go.

Straight answers

Know what you are buying before you install it.

Is SnapFlow PDR Automation a separate company?

No. SnapFlow PDR Automation is a product from Snap Flow Solutions. Snap Flow Solutions is responsible for the offer, communication and any separately scoped services.

Is this a monthly subscription?

No. Self Install is $297 one time and Installed for You is $699 one time. You remain responsible for your own HighLevel plan and phone, messaging, email, domain, AI and other provider charges.

Does the snapshot include a working AI phone receptionist?

It includes a disabled PDR Voice AI template and setup/testing guidance. It does not include an AI subscription, phone number, usage billing, business configuration, activation or ongoing management.

Will it work in my existing GoHighLevel account?

It can, but a clean sub-account is recommended. Existing accounts may contain overlapping fields, workflows, calendars or pipelines that require a careful compatibility review before anything is published.

Is installation or customization included?

Installation is included only with the $699 Installed for You option and is limited to the included snapshot assets and standard configuration. Migration, cleanup, new custom workflows and ongoing management are scoped separately.

Can I use it for more than one business?

The standard purchase covers one PDR company. HighLevel Assets Protection allows legitimate internal use inside the purchasing agency while restricting external redistribution and resale to another agency. Unrelated companies require separate licenses.

Does it automatically provide estimates or quotes?

No. The system organizes damage details and photos for review and can support inspection scheduling. Repairability, estimates, prices and final quotes are created by the repair business after review or an in-person inspection.
