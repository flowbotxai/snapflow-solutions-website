SnapFlow CRM questions & answers

Here’s how the subscription, setup, and any extra charges work.

What does the monthly subscription cover?

Your subscription gives you access to SnapFlow CRM. Explore the feature cards above to see what you can do. Your quote confirms what’s included in your plan.

What’s included in onboarding?

One-time onboarding can cover your starting pipeline, intake form, calendar, and follow-up templates. If a prebuilt setup fits your business, we can install it during onboarding. Your quote spells out what we’ll configure and the setup fee.

Are there extra fees for calls, texts, email, or AI?

Calls, texts, email, and applicable AI usage are billed separately from your subscription. Recurring phone number charges may also apply. We’ll explain which charges apply before you get started.

Can I add AI Employee, ongoing support, or custom work?

Yes. We can quote optional products such as AI Employee, ongoing support through SnapFlow Care, and services such as custom workflows, migrations, or integrations separately.
